AeroMind · Autonomous Multi Payload UAV

AeroMind is an autonomous modular multi payload aerial system with adaptive AI control architecture. The final year project explored how one aerial platform could support medical logistics, surveillance and disaster response applications.

OrganisationSymbiosis Skills & Professional University
RoleProject Team Member
PeriodB.Tech · Final year project

My contribution

  • Developed the modular payload arrangement for medical logistics, surveillance and disaster response use cases.
  • Evaluated payload mass, mounting constraints and airframe integration during prototype iterations.
  • Tested thermal imaging and person detection for surveillance, including aerial thermal capture and ground based RGB and thermal validation.
